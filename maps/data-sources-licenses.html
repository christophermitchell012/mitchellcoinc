Map rendering software

MitchellCo Tonight Score v2

For Map 26, only forecast-covered samples with Sun altitude at or below −18° count as astronomical darkness. The algorithm searches the next 24 hours for the best contiguous three-hour dark window. Its core score is 50% transformed annual VIIRS radiance, 35% NWS clear-sky percentage, and 15% Moon score. Moon interference uses illumination and altitude and becomes zero when the Moon is below the horizon. The final score is 90% of that best-window core plus 10% based on total forecast-covered astronomical-dark hours. This is a MitchellCo heuristic for comparative planning context, not an official astronomical forecast.

Map 27 · Your Compass Lies

NOAA NCEI / British Geological Survey · World Magnetic Model 2025

Global geomagnetic reference model

Map 27 uses the standard degree/order-12 WMM2025 coefficients locally in the browser. The coefficient file is stored same-origin at data/WMM2025.COF, and a normalized subset of NOAA's official validation vectors is stored at data/wmm2025-test-values.json. No magnetic-model service is called when a visitor clicks the map.

Dataset citation: NOAA NCEI Geomagnetic Modeling Team; British Geological Survey. 2024: World Magnetic Model 2025. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. doi:10.25921/aqfd-sd83. Accessed 2026-09-24.

  • WMM2025 product page, coefficients, citation and public-use notice
  • WMM accuracy, limitations, error model and compass blackout zones
  • Official WMM2025 test values

Reuse: NOAA states that the WMM source code is public domain, that its information/software may be used freely by the public, and that NOAA-produced material is not subject to U.S. copyright protection. This page identifies the incorporated U.S. Government material as required for cautious redistribution. No government or BGS endorsement is implied.

Validation: the local coefficient file is pinned to SHA-256 06791cd95faba7bdf4a709808f2715a53fe689b29c23b9886bc2196fa9b3eb13. Before enabling calculations, Map 27 evaluates six NOAA surface test vectors spanning 2025.0 and 2027.5 and checks field components plus secular variation against the published rounded values.

Interpretation: WMM models the long-wavelength internal magnetic field. NOAA states that crustal, upper-mantle, ionospheric and magnetospheric effects are not fully represented; a physical compass can also be disturbed by local magnetic material. Declination is therefore a model value, not a local magnetic survey. Map 27 flags NOAA's caution zone at 2,000–6,000 nT horizontal intensity and blackout/unreliable zone below 2,000 nT.

Map 61 · Global Water-Use Efficiency

FAO Data Explorer · SDG indicator 6.4.1 · Natural Earth

Dated country-level annual economic water-productivity indicator

Map 61 stores 15,805 finite country-level FAO SDG 6.4.1 observations for 184 ISO3 areas from 2000–2023, including overall, agriculture, industry and services estimates. FAO defines national water-use efficiency as the sum of major-sector efficiencies weighted by each sector's share of total water withdrawal, expressed as constant 2015 U.S. dollars of gross value added per cubic metre of water used. The source was retrieved anonymously on October 29, 2026; the linked workbook reported a July 23, 2026 last-modified date.

Generalized public-domain Natural Earth polygons draw all 184 represented areas. No value, boundary, activity or missing year is inferred. This is not a direct measure of conservation, water availability, scarcity, sustainability, physical irrigation efficiency, service quality, household access, drinking-water safety, affordability, infrastructure performance, forecast, operating recommendation or safety determination. Higher values can result from economic growth without reduced withdrawals.
