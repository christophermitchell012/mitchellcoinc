How to read this map

This is a dated census of qualifying fatal motor-vehicle traffic crashes, not every crash, injury, dangerous road or current hazard. A marker is a crash location, not a prediction of future risk.

Zoom and pan to inspect patterns. At closer zoom, click near a point for the NHTSA case number and coded context. Overlapping points can hide one another.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 36,127 crashes with usable coordinates and 39,073 fatalities across the 50 states and D.C. NHTSA's source file has 36,297 crash rows; 170 rows with unknown or unavailable coordinates are excluded. Locations and coded circumstances can contain reporting or geocoding error.

FARS includes a crash when a motor vehicle on a public trafficway is involved and a person dies within 30 days. It contains no names, addresses or Social Security numbers.
