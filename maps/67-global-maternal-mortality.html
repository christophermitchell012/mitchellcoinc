What this map measures

Switch among modeled maternal deaths per 100,000 live births, modeled lifetime risk of maternal death, and estimated maternal-death counts.

These are national modeled estimates, not live surveillance, individual risk predictions, health-system rankings, causal explanations, or medical advice.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot maps 194 countries or areas and source observations extend through 2023. Maternal deaths are difficult to measure and may be misclassified. The inter-agency model uses available national data and socioeconomic information; published ratios are not exact.
