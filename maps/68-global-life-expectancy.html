What this map measures

Switch among total, female, and male life expectancy at birth: the years a newborn would live if the year's mortality patterns remained constant throughout life.

These are national population estimates, not individual predictions, healthy-life-expectancy measures, health-system rankings, causal explanations, or medical advice.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot contains values for 217 countries or areas and maps 214. Source observations extend through 2024, but each popup shows its actual latest year. National averages hide subnational inequality, and modeled or interpolated series may not reflect sudden events precisely.
