What the map measures

Home value is the owner respondent’s estimate of how much the property would sell for if it were for sale. Values are 2020–2024 survey estimates in nominal dollars.

These are not appraisals, current asking or sale prices, tax assessments, equity, mortgage balances, affordability, housing condition, an individual property record, or financial advice.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County medians hide property and neighborhood differences.

Comparisons should consider the displayed 90% margin of error. The estimate covers owner-occupied housing units and excludes renter-occupied and vacant units. Generalized shapes are not legal boundaries.
