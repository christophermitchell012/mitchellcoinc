What this map measures

National estimates of the share of children receiving three DPT doses, one measles-containing-vaccine dose, or three hepatitis B doses. Switch among the three series.

These are national coverage estimates, not live immunization records, vaccine-effectiveness measures, local availability, proof of individual protection, or medical advice.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot maps 193 countries or areas and source observations extend through 2024. Each popup shows that country's actual latest source year. WHO and UNICEF reconcile administrative and survey evidence; cohort-size uncertainty and reporting differences can affect estimates.
