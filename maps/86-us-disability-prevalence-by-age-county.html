What the map measures

Each color shows the ACS table B18101 estimate of people with one or more reported disabilities as a share of the civilian noninstitutionalized population, overall or within a selected age group.

These are 2020–2024 survey estimates, not diagnoses, medical records, benefit eligibility, service availability, accommodation needs, severity, or an individual person's disability status. Margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide demographic, household and neighborhood differences.

ACS disability concepts are based on reported difficulty with selected functions and differ by age. Small age-group denominators can produce unstable rates. Generalized county shapes are not legal boundaries. Compare displayed counts as well as rates.
