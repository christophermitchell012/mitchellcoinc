What the map measures

Each color is the estimated share of the selected household group whose reported housing costs are at least 30% of household income. Units where the ratio could not be computed are excluded from the denominator.

This is a derived county estimate, not a live market measure or household determination. ACS estimates have sampling and nonsampling error; margins of error are not displayed here.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. Small denominators can produce extreme percentages. County averages hide neighborhood and household differences.

The 30% threshold is descriptive; it does not prove hardship, affordability, eligibility, displacement risk or cause. Generalized county shapes are not legal or parcel boundaries.
