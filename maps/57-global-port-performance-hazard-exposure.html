What is mapped

The snapshot joins 338 of 403 CPPI port rows to World Bank International Ports points: 331 by UN/LOCODE and 7 by a unique normalized name. Sixty-five rows without a defensible match are omitted.

A natural event at or near a port shows geographic coincidence only—not disruption, delay, damage, closure, capacity, cargo loss, route safety, or operating conditions.

Limits

CPPI compares observed vessel time in port and is a relative annual benchmark. It is not throughput, capacity, reliability, congestion now, a service guarantee, or a safety rating. Port coordinates come from a 2020 World Bank layer and may be approximate. EONET is an event catalog, not a warning service or exposure model.
