What the map measures

Each color is the estimated share of occupied housing units reporting the selected fuel as the fuel used most for heating. Source counts and all nine B25040 categories are preserved.

This is a dated survey estimate, not a live fuel-use, availability, price, equipment, energy-consumption or emissions measure. ACS estimates have sampling and nonsampling error; margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide household and neighborhood differences.

Solar is a heating-fuel response, not a rooftop-installation count. “No fuel used” does not establish habitability or the absence of every energy service. Generalized county shapes are not legal, parcel or utility-service boundaries.
