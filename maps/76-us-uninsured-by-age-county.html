What the map measures

Each color is the estimated share of the civilian noninstitutionalized population reporting no health-insurance coverage in the selected age group. Source counts and denominators are preserved.

This is a dated survey estimate, not current enrollment, care access, affordability, eligibility, health status, or individual coverage. ACS estimates have sampling and nonsampling error; margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide household and neighborhood differences.

The table covers the civilian noninstitutionalized population. Coverage status does not establish whether care is available, affordable, adequate, or used. Generalized county shapes are not legal or service boundaries.
