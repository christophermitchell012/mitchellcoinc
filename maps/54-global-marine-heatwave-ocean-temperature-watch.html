What is mapped

NOAA applies the Hobday marine-heatwave method to its gap-free daily CoralTemp sea-surface-temperature analysis. A marine heatwave occurs where SST exceeds the location's daily 90th-percentile threshold; intensity determines Categories 1–5.

This is a dated October 18, 2026 snapshot—not a live feed, forecast, measured local temperature, or operational advisory.

Limits

The global raster is 5 km (0.05°) and represents nighttime sea-surface-temperature analysis. Category does not equal degrees Celsius. NOAA's climatology ice mask is static and does not show daily sea ice. Do not use this map for navigation, fisheries, coral-bleaching response, or personal safety.
