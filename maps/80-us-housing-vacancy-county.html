What the map measures

The vacancy-rate view divides vacant units by all housing units using ACS table B25002. Each vacancy-type view divides its B25004 category by all vacant units, so the denominator changes when a vacancy type is selected. Counts and denominators are preserved.

This is a dated survey estimate, not a live listing, proof a unit is available or habitable, a price or affordability measure, a code-status or abandonment finding, or an individual property record. Margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide property and neighborhood differences.

Vacancy status reflects the Census Bureau survey categories and may include seasonal and occasional-use units. Generalized county shapes are not legal or parcel boundaries.
