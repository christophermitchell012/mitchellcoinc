What this map measures

The percentage of the selected population group with access to electricity, using the World Bank's SDG 7.1.1 national, urban and rural series.

This is not a utility-territory map or proof of service at an address. It does not measure affordability, reliability, hours of supply, voltage quality or fuel mix.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot maps 213 countries or areas. Source observations extend through 2024, but each popup shows the selected metric's actual latest year. National percentages hide local gaps and may include modeled estimates subject to revision.
