What the map measures

Each color is the estimated share of households in the selected ACS internet-access or subscription category. Source counts and the total-household denominator are preserved.

This is a dated survey estimate, not current service availability, speed, reliability, affordability, provider coverage, outage status, device access, digital skills, or address-level eligibility. Margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide household and neighborhood differences.

Subscription technologies can overlap unless the label says “only.” “Internet access without a subscription” and “no internet access” are distinct ACS responses. Generalized county shapes are not service boundaries.
