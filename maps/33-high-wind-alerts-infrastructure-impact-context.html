Infrastructure context

Wind alerts can describe potential effects on trees, power lines, roofs, high-profile vehicles, travel and marine operations. Those effects come from official alert text. This map does not contain an infrastructure inventory, predict outages, or estimate losses.

How to read this

Fact: shapes, alert categories, times and structured gusts come from NWS. Limit: conditions vary within every alert area. A missing structured gust is shown as unknown rather than inferred from prose. Follow the full warning and local instructions for decisions.

Source & runtime status

NOAA National Weather Service Alerts API and official affected-zone endpoints, requested directly in your browser. Anonymous and keyless; no alert snapshot is bundled. Zone fallback is capped at 80 unique zones per load. Source/license notes · NWS API documentation
