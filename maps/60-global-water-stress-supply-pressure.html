What the indicator means

SDG 6.4.2 is total freshwater withdrawal by major economic sectors as a percentage of renewable freshwater resources after environmental water requirements are considered.

This country-level annual ratio is not a live supply gauge, local shortage forecast, household-access measure, drinking-water assessment, allocation recommendation, or safety determination.

Coverage and limits

The same-origin snapshot contains 16,487 country observations for 182 ISO3 areas. Generalized Natural Earth polygons draw 180; French Guiana and Réunion remain counted but are not separately drawn. Country averages can hide basin, seasonal and local differences. Values above 100% are retained as published.
