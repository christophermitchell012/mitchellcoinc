Model and resolution

CPC estimates soil moisture with a one-layer hydrological model driven by observed precipitation and temperature on a global 0.5° grid. The model uses a spatially constant 760 mm maximum holding capacity.

The percentile is calculated against CPC's 1979–2019 modeled record. Anomaly is departure from CPC climatology; check the official product legend for the active baseline and units.

Use limits

This is modeled land-surface context—not an in-ground sensor reading, parcel analysis, crop-loss estimate, drought declaration, forecast, fire prediction, or safety guidance. Rapid drying can contribute to flash drought, but one image alone does not establish onset, cause, impact, or severity.
