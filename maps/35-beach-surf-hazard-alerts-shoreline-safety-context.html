Shoreline context

Official alerts may describe dangerous currents, breaking waves, surf height, beach erosion or hazards near piers and jetties. This map preserves that context but does not report lifeguard staffing, beach closures or conditions between observation points.

How to read this

Fact: alert categories, times, descriptions and shapes come from NWS. Limit: water and shoreline conditions can change quickly and vary inside every alert area. Read the full official alert and follow local beach-safety guidance.

Source & runtime status

NOAA National Weather Service Alerts API and official affected-zone endpoints, requested directly in your browser. Anonymous and keyless; no alert snapshot is bundled. Geometry loading uses four-request concurrency, caching and an 80-zone ceiling. Source/license notes · NWS API documentation
