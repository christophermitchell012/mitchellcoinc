What this map measures

Renewable internal freshwater is river flow and groundwater recharge generated by precipitation within a country. Switch between cubic metres per person and total billion cubic metres per year.

This is not total actual renewable water, live supply, storage, withdrawals, utility service, household access, drinking-water quality, or a safety determination.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot contains both measures for 183 mapped countries. Most latest observations are for 2022; every popup shows its source year. Estimates may refer to different underlying assessment periods and national averages hide basin, seasonal and local variation.
