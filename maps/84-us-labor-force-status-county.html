What the map measures

Each color shows one ACS table B23025 estimate for people age 16 and older: labor-force participation, employed people as a share of that population, or unemployed people as a share of the civilian labor force.

These are 2020–2024 survey estimates, not current monthly labor statistics, job openings, wages, job quality, underemployment, discouraged-worker counts, benefit eligibility or individual employment records. Margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide demographic, household and neighborhood differences.

ACS definitions and reference periods differ from the monthly Current Population Survey and Local Area Unemployment Statistics. Generalized county shapes are not legal boundaries. Compare displayed counts as well as rates.
