What the map measures

Each color is the estimated share of workers age 16 or older using the selected usual means of transportation to work. Counts reconcile to the B08301 total.

This is a dated survey estimate, not live traffic, trip, congestion, travel-time, emissions, employment, or service-availability data. ACS estimates have sampling and nonsampling error; margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide worker, household and neighborhood differences.

“Worked from home” is a commute-mode response, not a count of all remote-work days. Public transportation excludes taxi and ride-hailing. Generalized county shapes are not legal or service boundaries.
