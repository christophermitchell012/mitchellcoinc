What this map measures

NASA POWER supplies long-term monthly and annual averages for downward all-sky solar irradiance and modeled mean wind speed 50 meters above the surface.

These are coarse-grid climatology estimates, not site measurements, forecasts, engineering designs, energy-production guarantees, or proof that a location is suitable for a project.

Resolution and limits

Solar values represent roughly 1° source cells; wind values roughly 0.5° × 0.625° cells. Nearby clicks can therefore return the same grid value. Buildings, trees, terrain, turbine choice, panel orientation, shading, access, interconnection, permitting, economics and environmental constraints are not evaluated.
