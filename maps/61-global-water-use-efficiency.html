What the indicator means

SDG 6.4.1 expresses gross value added per unit of water used. The overall country value weights agriculture, industry and service-sector efficiencies by their shares of total water withdrawal.

Higher values do not by themselves prove lower withdrawals, conservation, sustainable supply, good service, or safe water. FAO reports that recent global gains were mainly driven by economic growth rather than reduced withdrawals.

Coverage and limits

The same-origin snapshot contains 15,805 observations for 184 ISO3 areas. Generalized Natural Earth polygons draw all 184 represented areas. Country averages can hide basin, seasonal and local differences. Sector values are descriptive economic-productivity indicators, not performance targets.
