What this map measures

The probability per 1,000 live births that a newborn would die before age five if subject to the age-specific mortality rates of the indicated year. Switch among overall, female, and male estimates.

These are national modeled estimates, not death counts, live surveillance, individual risk predictions, causal explanations, or medical advice.

Coverage and limits

The dated snapshot maps 195 countries or areas and source observations extend through 2024. Each popup shows the selected measure's actual latest year. Survey, model and extrapolation uncertainty can be larger where source data are sparse.
