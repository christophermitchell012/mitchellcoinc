What the map measures

Each color is the estimated share of all housing units in the selected year-built category from ACS table B25034. “Since 2010” combines the 2010–2019 and 2020-or-later categories; “before 1960” combines the 1950s, 1940s, and 1939-or-earlier categories. Counts and denominators are preserved.

This is a dated survey estimate, not a building inspection, condition or safety rating, renovation history, code-compliance finding, lead or asbestos assessment, energy-efficiency measure, or individual property record. Margins of error are not displayed.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County averages hide property and neighborhood differences.

The source reports when structures were originally built, not their current condition or the age of later additions and systems. Generalized county shapes are not legal or parcel boundaries.
