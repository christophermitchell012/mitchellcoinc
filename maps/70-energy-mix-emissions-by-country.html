Renewables share of electricity

What the measures mean

Electricity shares divide generation into renewables, fossil fuels and nuclear power. CO₂ per person is territorial fossil-fuel and industrial emissions divided by population.

Electricity mix is not total primary energy. CO₂ excludes land-use change and imported-goods emissions. Different measures can have different latest years for the same country.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot maps 199 areas with electricity-mix values and 204 with emissions values. Each popup shows its actual source year. National averages hide subnational differences and do not establish household access, grid reliability, affordability, project suitability, policy effectiveness or individual carbon footprints.
