Accuracy, privacy and source limits

A polygon overlap is a candidate match, not proof that a provider serves a parcel, building or account. Boundaries can be incomplete, generalized, overlapping or stale. Confirm service and rates directly with the provider.

Your typed address is sent to a public geocoder. The resulting coordinates—not the address text—are sent to the three territory services. No address is stored by this page.

The electric source reports 2020 data. The gas layer metadata reports a February 25, 2021 edit. Water records contain source-specific dates and methods. Some places may return multiple or no matches.
