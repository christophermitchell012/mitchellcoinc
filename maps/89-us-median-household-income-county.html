What the map measures

Median household income is the midpoint of the ACS household-income distribution, expressed in 2024 inflation-adjusted dollars. Relative margin of error divides the published 90% margin of error by the estimate.

These are 2020–2024 survey estimates, not individual or current income, wages, wealth, disposable income, purchasing power, cost of living, economic mobility, tax records, benefit eligibility, or financial advice.

Coverage and limits

The snapshot contains 3,222 counties or county equivalents in the 50 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico. County medians hide household and neighborhood differences.

Comparisons should consider the displayed margin of error. Inflation adjustment permits comparison within this release but does not make the values current beyond the survey vintage. Generalized shapes are not legal boundaries.
