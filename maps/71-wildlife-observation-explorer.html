How to read this map

Markers are submitted occurrence records, not live animal tracking. Coordinates may be obscured, rounded or uncertain. No result does not prove absence, and a record does not prove current presence.

Only CC0 and CC BY occurrence records are displayed. Photos are intentionally omitted because media can carry a different licence.

Sources and limits

Each query requests at most 30 records per source. iNaturalist is limited to Research Grade observations. GBIF returns coordinate-bearing PRESENT records in a bounding box, then the browser keeps only points inside the chosen radius. Duplicates can exist across sources.
